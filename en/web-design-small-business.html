The process

How you get
to a live site

Five steps, and at the end of each you know where we are and what comes next.

01

Free discovery call

You tell us who your customers are and what they ask you most often, and we listen. By the end of the call it’s clear what the new site needs to do.

02

Tailored proposal

You get an itemised quote: what gets built, what doesn’t, and by when. The quote also sets when to send us the photos, the list of your services and the access details, and the deadline guarantee is tied to that.

03

Starting out

Once the deposit arrives, we schedule the project and the work begins.

04

Design

We show you the design, and you shape it. Only then do we start building.

05

Build and handover

We build it, test it on every device and launch it. Want to add more later? We carry on from there.

A deadline guarantee, as long as the materials reach us on time. Three rounds of changes.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

How long does a website take?

We set the exact deadline in the quote, based on the scope and the materials. We guarantee it, as long as the materials we ask for reach us on time.

It's a lot of money right now.

We understand: a website is expensive if it brings in nothing. In the quote you see item by item where your money goes, and if we think you don’t need something, we’ll tell you.

I already have a website.

If your current site brings in enquiries, you may not need a new one at all. In the free first conversation we look at what can be kept. Your content and your domain stay.

I don’t have time for this right now.

We carry the work: from you we only need the photos, the list of your services and the access details, plus your approval at the key points.

Where do you work?

We work across Hungary, in Hungarian, English and German. Design and handover happen on video calls, but if the project calls for it, we come to you in person.

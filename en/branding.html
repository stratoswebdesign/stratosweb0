The difference

A logo is a mark.
An identity
is a system.

On Monday morning the new flyers arrive from the printer, and somehow they don’t look like your website. Your posts look different again, and your business card could belong to another company altogether.

Maybe you think that’s a detail as long as you do good work, and you’re partly right. But good work doesn’t show on its own: people who don’t know you yet judge it by how you look. A brand identity pulls these pieces into one system, from the business card to the website.

The process

How your
identity is made

We don't put a finished logo in front of you and say “there, that's you now”. We work out together what makes you different.

Free discovery call

You tell us who your customers are, what sets you apart from the others and where the brand will live, and we ask questions and listen.

Tailored proposal

You get an itemised quote: what gets made, in which formats, and by when. The quote also sets when we need your feedback and the materials, and the deadline guarantee is tied to that.

Directions and decision

Once the deposit is in, we present the directions. You choose, and you shape the final one.

Turning it into a system

The chosen direction becomes a complete identity: logo versions, colour codes, typefaces, image style and brand guidelines.

Handover and roll-out

You get every file. If you like, the same team carries it on to the website and the ads.

A deadline guarantee, as long as the materials reach us on time. Three rounds of changes.

Help

Frequently asked
questions

The website comes first. Can't the identity wait?

It can wait if you already have an established look. If you don’t, the colours, typefaces and image style have to be decided while the site is designed anyway. If you like, the same team handles the identity design and the website in one project, so you don’t pay twice for the same decision.

I already have an identity. Do we redesign it or build on it?

It depends on how strong the existing basis is. If your logo and colours work well, it’s often enough to build on them and turn them into a system, and we’ll tell you so. If it’s inconsistent or dated, a redesign is the better choice.

I'm a small company. Isn't this a luxury for me?

It isn’t a luxury, but we don’t promise miracles either: a brand identity doesn’t bring in orders by itself. A consistent identity helps people who don’t know you yet to trust you sooner, because they see how you look before they see your work.

How do you price it?

With a one-off, itemised project fee that we give you up front, after the free first conversation. The price mainly depends on whether you need just a logo or a full identity, and for how many uses it is made. If you also need a website, the two can be done in one project.

How long does it take, and what do I need to provide?

We set the deadline in the quote and guarantee it, as long as your feedback and the materials reach us on time. What we mostly need from you is to hear who your customers are and what sets you apart.

Make your brand recognisable.

Write a few sentences about what you do and whether you already have a logo. In a short, free conversation we listen, and we tell you what should stay and what we would change.

Where orders get lost

On the bus, someone puts two products in the basket on their phone. At checkout comes the delivery fee, then a compulsory sign-up, then an error message. They close the tab.

All you see is a weaker month. If you think your products aren’t the problem, you may well be right: the customer got stuck at checkout.

Before launch we run through payment and delivery ourselves, so your first real customer isn’t greeted by an error message.

The system

What goes into
building an
online store?

Products

Catalogue and product data

Payment and delivery

Card, cash on delivery, bank transfer, courier, parcel point. Delivery costs already show in the basket.

The platform

We choose
the platform
for your shop.

You’ve probably heard recommendations for this or that shop system, and everyone said something different. We first look at how many products you have, in how many variants, and which software the shop needs to connect to. Replacing the wrong platform later costs more than choosing well at the start.

We don’t promise that a new shop will bring in more customers on its own. Visitors come from search and ads, and the decision also depends on the product and the price. The shop’s job is to make sure that someone who has decided doesn’t get stuck at checkout.

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

What does an online store cost?

It depends on how many products and variants you have, and which software the shop must connect to. After the free first conversation you get an itemised quote with a one-off project fee.

I already have an online shop.

You may not need a new one: if the shop is good but few people find it, you need SEO. If not, product data and orders can usually move across.

I don't have time to upload the products.

You don’t have to do it alone: we help you get the photos, descriptions and variants in order, and from you we need the decisions. If you already have them, we finish sooner.

Do I get an admin interface?

Yes, cut down to the few things you will actually do: adding products, changing prices and stock, handling orders, coupons. Not a twenty-item panel of which you use three.

Who is responsible for legal compliance?

A lawyer is responsible for what the legal texts say, not us. We build in the places for the mandatory content (terms, withdrawal notice, privacy, cookies) and tell you if anything is missing.

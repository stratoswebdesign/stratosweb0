With us, most projects start with the website. If you don’t have a site yet, running ads is throwing money away: after the click there’s nowhere to land. If you have one but nobody finds it, you don’t necessarily need a new site; SEO may be enough.

03Combinations

What actually works together?

You don’t have to order everything at once, but the order matters.

Identity, then website
Colours, typefaces and the logo have to be decided while the site is designed anyway. If the brand identity only comes afterwards, you pay for the design twice.
Website, then advertising
With ads you buy traffic. If the site doesn’t convince, the visitor goes back, and you’ve paid for the click anyway.
Website and SEO together
Most of SEO comes down to the site’s structure, speed and copy. During the build that’s work; afterwards it’s a rebuild.
Website, then monthly SEO
The work doesn’t stop at handover: whoever built the site carries it on, with a monthly report.
Advertising only
If your site already brings in quote requests and, measurably, only traffic is missing, we don’t need to touch the site: ads are enough.
Identity only
Your site works, but your brand hasn’t grown with the company. Brand identity design is a complete project on its own, without a website too.

04The proposal

How is the proposal put together?

We don’t publish a price list: until we know what you need, any figure would be a guess. What a website’s price depends on, we’ve set out in our article on website prices.

Free discovery call

We talk through what you do, who your customers are and which service would help most right now. If you already have a site, we also look at what can be kept.

Scope

We agree how many pages you need, which features, who writes the copy and who supplies the images: the price depends mostly on these.

Written proposal

We write down the itemised price, the schedule and what’s left out. The proposal is valid for 14 days.

Go or no go

The proposal doesn’t commit you to anything: if you say no, we leave it there. If you say yes, the work starts once the deposit arrives.

The website is a one-off project fee. You can add SEO and maintenance alongside it as a monthly partnership.

A deadline guarantee, as long as the materials reach us on time. Three rounds of changes.
